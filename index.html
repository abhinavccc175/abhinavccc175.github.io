The pathway

Four stages of school

Most students follow this broad route. New choices open up as they get older.

01

École maternelle

Ages 3–6 · Preschool

Children learn through play, activities, and daily routines.

02

École élémentaire

Ages 6–11 · Primary

Students build skills in reading, writing, maths, and more.

03

Collège & lycée

Ages 11–18 · Secondary

Students study core subjects, then choose a path that fits their interests.

04

Higher education

After the bac

Options include university, specialist schools, and vocational training.
